Marine Pay — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Marine Pay app (customers and shops). Last updated: November 2026.

What we keep

  • Your name, email, and mobile number.
  • For shops: shop name, UPI ID, address, menu or items, and photos the shop adds.
  • Records of orders, bookings, bills, payments you mark as paid, and cashback rewards.
  • For home delivery only: the address and location you choose to share.

Data is stored securely on Google Firebase.

Why we keep it

  • To sign you in and run your account.
  • To tell shops about your orders, bookings and payments.
  • To run cashback rewards, Top 10 and Refer & earn.
  • To stop fraud and misuse.

Who sees it

A shop sees only what it needs for your order, booking or payment. We never sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

Payments

UPI payments go from your UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay and others) straight to the shop's bank. Marine Pay never sees or stores your bank details or UPI PIN. Rewards are promotional discounts; they are won, not bought, and can't be turned into cash.

Phone permissions

  • Camera — only to scan QR codes.
  • Location — only when you ask for home delivery.
  • Storage / photos — only to save a QR to your gallery or to pick a photo you choose.
  • Notifications — only if you allow them; you can turn them off any time.

Reward notes you save are kept only on your phone.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Delete my account → enter your password → type DELETE.

Without the app: email vr9998777@gmail.com from the email you signed up with, subject "Delete my Marine Pay account".

Your account and data are removed within 30 days, except records the law asks us to keep. Rewards left in a deleted account end.
